Software

NeuroXplorer 1.02020 – 2022
An extensible framework for modeling neuromorphic architectures from a generalized template. Supports cycle-accurate architectural simulation and high-level dataflow analysis, with an optimization engine that weighs hardware metrics (energy, throughput, latency) against SNN metrics such as inter-spike interval distortion and spike disorder.
SpiNeMap2019 – 2020
A design methodology for mapping SNNs to crossbar-based neuromorphic hardware while minimizing spike latency and energy. It partitions an SNN into synapse clusters (SpiNeCluster) and then places those clusters onto hardware (SpiNePlacer).
PyCARL2018 – 2020
A PyNN-based Python interface to CARLsim, the GPU-accelerated SNN simulator, for hardware-software co-simulation. It integrates cycle-accurate models of neuromorphic hardware such as DYNAP-SE to estimate latency and energy.
